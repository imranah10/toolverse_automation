LINKEDIN POSTING TIPS
--------------------
• Upload all 6 slides in order.
• Use the first slide as the visual hook; don't repeat the entire headline in the caption.
• Keep the caption professional and discussion-oriented.
• Invite comments about what users look for before buying a product.
• Avoid claiming that the AI assessment is infallible or guarantees the correct purchase decision.
• Avoid fabricated reviews, ratings, testimonials or performance statistics.
• Make sure any privacy/local-processing claim matches the actual implementation.
• Check the destination URL before publishing.
• Use a small set of relevant hashtags rather than a long hashtag block.
